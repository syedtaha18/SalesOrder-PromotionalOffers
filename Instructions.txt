Track 1: Sales Order with “Promotional Offers”
Build a lightweight sales order application using SAP Build Apps that showcases the power of no-code/low-code platforms in enhancing sales workflows.  The application must address the following key functionalities:  

Streamlined Order Creation:
-  Quickly select customers, products and quantities with a user-friendly interface.
- The Customer data entity must include fields like Customer ID, Name,  Country,  Contact No., Email etc
- The Product data entity must include fields like Product ID, Name, Category, Price etc
Integrated Promotions Display:
- The Promotions data entity must include fields like Promotion ID, Product ID, Name, Price etc
- The local database dynamically fetches and displays relevant promotions for the selected products.
Seamless Invoice Generation
- Finalize orders and generate invoices seamlessly, ensuring a smooth checkout process.
- The invoice must contain information regarding the customer, product and promotion availed.
